What you've heard about agency prices

Six things clients keep telling us in first calls — and our honest answers.

A good website costs €2,000.

That's not wrong — simple templates go for €500–€2,000. Our Start package (€990) sits right in the middle, but: custom design instead of a template, GDPR setup included, the source code is yours, no vendor lock-in.

Day rates are more honest than fixed prices.

Day rates shift the risk onto you. A fixed price means we carry the scoping risk, not you. That's why we take more time in the quoting phase.

Hosting always comes with the offer.

No. We host in your name, in an Austrian data centre, on your contract. You can switch at any time — without us taking “our” thing back.

WordPress is cheaper because it's open source.

Plugins, updates and security patches cost time that you pay for. A custom CMS is, over a 3-year TCO , usually cheaper — and nobody reads your credentials in public forums.

Agencies don't let clients leave.

With us, you own the code, the Git repo, the infrastructure. If you want to switch, we send the handover report — at no cost.

“From €X” ends up meaning double.

With us, the quoted price is the invoiced price. Fixed means fixed — no hidden hours, no extras without written approval.

Payment, cancellation, contracts

Nine questions almost every new client asks — and our honest answers before you have to ask.

Do I have to book hosting and maintenance with you?

No. You pay for the website once, at a fixed price. Hosting & maintenance are optional: after launch you decide whether we take care of it (from €39 / month) or you host it yourself — then we hand over the code, access and instructions.

How exactly does payment work?

50% at project start, 50% at launch. Payment due within 14 days net, by bank transfer or card payment via Stripe. No 100% upfront payment, no final invoice three months later.

How fast can I go live?

Start package: 4–5 weeks from signing. Pro: 5–6 weeks. Signature: 6–8 weeks. Provided the content (copy, logo, photos) is ready in week 1.

What happens after the 10 early-adopter slots?

Then the regular prices apply: €1,290 / €2,190 / €3,290. Existing clients keep their rate — for follow-up projects and maintenance.

Can I switch between Start and Pro?

Yes, any time. Upgrading to Pro costs the difference (€800) plus the extra features (language +€300, analytics +€150).

Can I cancel maintenance at any time?

Yes. All Care plans can be cancelled monthly at the end of the month. No minimum contract. When you cancel, you receive a full handover report with all credentials.

What about copyright and source code?

With the final invoice, the source code passes entirely into your ownership (buyout clause in the contract). You receive the Git repository, all design files and the infrastructure definition. No vendor lock-in.

How exactly does the 30-day money-back guarantee work?

Within 30 calendar days of launch you can request cancellation in writing. We refund all paid instalments within 14 banking days. You keep the source code.

Which contracts do I sign in the end?

Two documents: (1) a service contract with scope, milestones and price, (2) an optional maintenance contract. All documents come as PDF drafts for review before you accept the quote. No clauses in the small print.

Before you write

The questions we get most often — answered briefly and honestly.

How fast do you reply?

On weekdays within 24 hours by email. If we're busy, we'll at least send a quick “seen it, I'll get back to you by Thursday”. No ghosting.

Does the first call cost anything?

No. 30 minutes by video. If it becomes clear we're not a fit, you get an honest recommendation — not an invoice.

Do you take on small projects?

Our packages start at €990 (Start). In a short call we'll figure out which package fits best.

Do I get the code and Git access?

Yes, from day one. The repo is in your name, infrastructure access stays with you. No agency lock-in.

Which languages do you work in?

Projects usually run in German. Documentation, code comments and interfaces can be delivered in multiple languages (DE/EN/TR).

What happens to my data?

Your name, email and message end up in our inbox only — no sharing, no tracking pixels, no newsletter lists.

— Frequently asked

Before you write.

How fast do you reply?+

By email within 24 hours on weekdays. If we are tight on capacity, you still get a short note with a concrete reply time. No ghosting.

Does the first call cost anything?+

No. 30 minutes by video. If it is clear in the end that we are not the right fit, you get an honest recommendation — no invoice.

Do you also take small projects?+

Our packages start at €990 (Start). A quick call will help us find the right package for you.

Do I get the code and Git access?+

Yes, from day one. The repo sits in your name, and the infrastructure access stays with you. No agency lock-in.

Which languages do you work in?+

Projects usually run in German. Documentation, code comments and interfaces can be delivered in multiple languages (DE/EN/TR).

What happens to my data?+

Your name, email and message go only to our inbox — no sharing, no tracking pixels, no newsletter lists.
